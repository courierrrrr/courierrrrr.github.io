About

I’m Georgia, and I came to tech by a slightly unusual route. I studied Economics at the Aristotle University of Thessaloniki, while working full time as a video editor for various clients, and then I pursued a Master’s degree in Applied Informatics. I'm particularly interested in medical informatics, a field that became personal for me as someone living with a chronic illness. That experience led me to my thesis, where I apply machine learning to clinical data, and it drives my interest in how technology can improve healthcare outcomes.
